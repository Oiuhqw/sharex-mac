ShareX for macOS 0.1.1 (build 2)
macOS 14 or later. Universal: Apple Silicon and Intel.

Drag ShareX.app to Applications, then open it. ShareX lives in the menu bar.
Allow Screen Recording in System Settings > Privacy & Security, then quit and reopen ShareX.

This build is ad-hoc signed with Hardened Runtime. It is NOT Apple-notarized.
The project has no Developer ID certificate. A downloaded copy may require
System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway after the first launch attempt.
Check ShareX.dmg.sha256 from the same build before installing.

If macOS says the app "is damaged" or refuses to open it, clear the download
quarantine flag after copying it to Applications, then open it again:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/ShareX.app

When updating an earlier ad-hoc build, remove the old ShareX entry from Screen
Recording permissions, add the updated /Applications/ShareX.app, and reopen it.

Changes in 0.1.1:
- Clipboard and PNG file export succeed or fail independently.
- Failed outputs show Retry and Save As; the screenshot remains available while recovering.
- Successful outputs are preserved when retrying, without duplicate files.
- The success sound plays only when every requested output succeeds.
- App icon added, and invalid relative save-folder paths rejected.
- Magnifier (loupe) appears on the first frame and follows the cursor even when ShareX is not the active app.
- Esc, Return, tool hotkeys and typed text reach the capture overlay while another app stays in front.
- Blur and pixelate strength cannot go below 8 pt, so small text stays unreadable.
- Smoother blur while dragging (reduced-resolution preview; the saved image is full resolution).

Native automated tests cover capture/editor/export flows and injected export failures.
Hosted CI cannot fully verify physical hotkeys, real multi-monitor/Retina hardware,
or the interactive permission prompts on your Mac.
